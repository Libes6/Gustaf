# Subscriptions

**Settings → Subscriptions** keeps one list of everything you pay for, such as Claude, ChatGPT,
Cursor, Antigravity, Telegram Premium, or Spotify, with the amount and the next charge.

The list is stored on the environment selected in Settings, so every device connected to that
environment sees the same list. Providers do not publish renewal dates, so you enter each one
yourself.

## Add a subscription

Choose **Add subscription** and enter the amount, the currency code (such as `USD` or `RUB`), how
often it charges, and the date of any charge you know. Later charges repeat from that date, so
"5 USD every 3 months, paid on November 10" is an amount of 5, every 3 months, with November 10 as
the known charge. A subscription billed on the 31st charges on the last day of shorter months.

Turn off **Recurring** for a one-time payment. Turn on **Cancelled** for a subscription you stopped:
it shows no next charge and does not count in the totals.

**Totals** shows recurring spending per month and per year for each currency. Amounts in different
currencies are not converted or added together.

Set **Mark as due soon** to flag a subscription in the list a few days before its charge. Gustaf does
not send a notification for it.

## Ask an agent

Agents can list, add, edit, and delete subscriptions, for example "add Antigravity, 5 USD every 3
months, paid on Nov 10". Adding, editing, or deleting needs an agent in full-access mode, the same
as changing other environment settings.

Never enter a card number. **Payment method** is only a label, such as "Visa ··1234".

Subscriptions are not available in the mobile app yet.
